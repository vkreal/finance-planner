## Contract v104 (2026-10-30): Round-14 pricing-parity fix F5 — January-paid capital-gain distributions keep their long-term-gain character in December

All user-entered data, no external source. The one fix in this build (finding F5, High, from the settled round-14 pricing-parity audit; no other round-14 finding is touched; contract v100's F2, v101's F4, v102's F1, and v103's F3 are built on, not redone). THE DEFECT: `decemberCaseInputs` added the whole January-paid stray total (`strayJanPaid`) to ordinary dividends with only a qualified-dividend split, and the state carried no capital-gain character field — so a fund distribution the fund designated as a capital gain dividend was priced as an ordinary dividend. December had the year right and the character wrong: 26 U.S.C. §852(b)(7) deems a distribution declared by a regulated investment company in October, November, or December and actually paid during January to have been received by each shareholder on December 31 of the declaration year, and a capital gain dividend so designated is treated as a long-term capital gain under §852(b)(3). The qualified split mitigated the federal stacking only partially and could never fix Washington, whose capital-gains excise prices long-term gains and never dividends, so a January-paid capital-gain distribution never entered the Washington base at all. THE FIX (character split only — no new tax math): the December state gains one field, `strayJanPaidCapGainAmount`, the capital-gain-distribution PORTION of the January-paid total, alongside the existing `strayJanPaid` total and `strayJanPaidQualifiedAmount`. In `decemberCaseInputs` the portion is clamped to the total, added to the capital result so it rides the same `totalGain` → `stockSold`/`stockBasis` → `ltcg` path `remCapDist` and `strayCapDist` already use (that path is unchanged), and REMOVED from ordinary dividends, so no dollar is counted twice — total income and MAGI are unchanged by the split, only the character moves. The qualified-dividend split now applies to the ordinary remainder only: the qualified portion is clamped to the remainder (total minus the capital-gain portion), never to the whole total. The plan, Playground, and work-years pricing are untouched (they already route long-term gains correctly). THE CONSOLE (client December.tsx): the January-paid stray item gains an "Of which capital-gain distributions" field beside the qualified field, with a plain-words note: a distribution declared in October–December and paid in January counts as received on December 31 of the earlier year, and a capital-gain distribution is a long-term gain, not an ordinary dividend — enter that part so it is taxed as a gain. RELATIONSHIP TO THE LEGACY PROVENANCE MACHINERY (verified in source, decemberCore.ts): the `legacyJanPaidSplitUnconfirmed` flag and the save derivation's `janPaidCharacter` edit flag govern ONLY the qualified-amount provenance — the flag says a stored January-paid amount predates the qualified split and asks the user to confirm the qualified portion, it changes no pricing, and it clears only when the qualified field is edited. The new capital-gain field neither reads nor writes that flag: editing the capital-gain portion does not clear it (the qualified portion may still be unconfirmed), the flag's derivation on pre-format-3 blobs still keys only on the qualified key's absence or $0 default, and a blob's flag state prices exactly as before — the two splits are independent portions of the same total, each clamped in the assembly, so they cannot conflict or double-count.
